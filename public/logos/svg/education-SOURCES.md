# Educational vector artwork

- Element identities: atomic numbers 1–118, symbols and names follow the [IUPAC periodic table](https://iupac.org/what-we-do/periodic-table-of-elements/). The pinned factual list is `scripts/elements.json`, transcribed from [Periodic-Table-JSON](https://github.com/Bowserinator/Periodic-Table-JSON). No atomic weights, speculative element 119 or disputed family classifications are included. Periods provide the filters. Both US and IUPAC spellings of aluminum/aluminium and cesium/caesium are accepted.
- Element letter and number outlines come from DejaVu Sans. Math uses DejaVu Serif outlines (with the familiar curved lowercase pi retained from DejaVu Sans) pinned in `scripts/math_glyphs.json`; `scripts/build_math_glyphs.py FONT_PATH` can refresh those outlines with fontTools installed. See `education-LICENSE.txt` for the Bitstream Vera license and public-domain DejaVu additions. Glyphs are baked as paths, so exported images and offline games do not depend on installed fonts.
- Shapes, space illustrations and science figures are original SVG geometry in `scripts/build_education.py` and `scripts/education_artwork.py`. Two-dimensional outlines retain exact straight edges and curves; three-dimensional solids have opaque visible faces instead of see-through wireframes. Planet surface features are simplified identification cues (continents, craters, cloud bands, rings and Pluto's Tombaugh region), with continuous spherical lighting. Sun and Moon are now full surface studies. These are illustrations, not scale diagrams or scientific surface maps. Science figures use thinner outlines, glass and liquid gradients, menisci, cell organelles and mechanical details. The atom is an orbital schematic, not a literal electron-orbit model; the cell is a generic animal cell.
- Shading consists of native SVG paths and gradient stops in the remix palette, with no raster textures, blur filters, remote references or live text. Unused gradient definitions are removed before writing, so palette coverage reflects the painted geometry. The 14 existing 2D outline alternates retain their IDs and artwork.
- `education-manifest.json` records SHA-256 checksums for every generated file. Run `npm run teams:education` to reproduce the assets and catalog offline with Python 3.
- All collections are logo sources. Existing NFL, NBA and college teams remain the only color donors. Element tiles show the symbol and atomic number, with a symbol-only alternate; neither version spells the element's name.

# Landmark artwork

The collection keeps 40 stable landmark IDs. Twenty-four retain their pinned [Openclipart](https://openclipart.org/) vectors (CC0); sixteen formerly sparse illustrations are replaced by original architectural studies in `scripts/landmark_artwork.py`: Statue of Liberty, Golden Gate Bridge, Brooklyn Bridge, Christ the Redeemer, Tower Bridge, Leaning Tower of Pisa, Stonehenge, Saint Basil's Cathedral, Mont-Saint-Michel, Taj Mahal, Petronas Towers, Itsukushima torii, Moai, Oriental Pearl Tower, Burj Khalifa and Tokyo Skytree. These are stylized architectural illustrations, not measured elevations. The source records explicitly distinguish these original drawings from imported artwork; the former Openclipart record is retained as `previousSource` for provenance.

Every asset is pinned in `scripts/brand_artwork_sources.json`. Generated sources have an explicit allowlisted `generator` and a checksum of the generated SVG; the regular importer still validates vectors, palettes and output checksums. `npm run logos:landmarks` and `npm run teams:landmarks` remain the refresh/build commands. `--force` can reproduce the sixteen original studies offline; imported originals still require their upstream sources when forced.

# Character studies

Ten new name-free alternates in `scripts/character_artwork.py` supplement existing artwork: Spider-Man's webbed mask, Deadpool's mask portrait, Darth Vader's helmet, the Mandalorian's armored helmet, Thor's hammer, Captain America's heater shield, Creeper's voxel head, Mario's fire flower, Pokémon's Great Ball and Pikachu's face. These are original project illustrations of existing characters and props, not official logo files; character and trademark rights remain with their respective owners. Existing primaries, wordmark flags and alternate IDs are unchanged.

After editing and visually reviewing original drawings, run `python3 scripts/pin_illustrations.py`, then the normal `logos:landmarks`, `teams:landmarks`, `logos:characters` and `teams:characters` commands. The pin script updates only the original studies; refresh rejects a mismatched generated-source checksum.

# Additional state and NHL artwork

State flags and silhouettes are pinned in `scripts/brand_artwork_sources.json`, including original Wikimedia source pages, source checksums and explicit crops. All 50 states have a flag and an independently cropped silhouette, including Rhode Island. Oregon also has its beaver reverse. Existing state IDs are preserved, including `FLAG-CALI`.

All 50 states also offer a `flag-map` alternate. Texas retains its existing imported flag map; the other 49 are composed by `scripts/state_flag_artwork.py` from the exact checked-in flag and silhouette. Native SVG clipping retains the original state boundaries, islands and separate peninsulas, with transparency outside the state. Flags retain their aspect ratio: striped designs cover the outline, while uniform-field flags fit over a matching field color. Reviewed placements keep Alaska's stars on its mainland and Michigan's seal on its lower peninsula; Georgia, Nevada and Minnesota retain their left-side emblems. Ohio's burgee uses a white field behind its notch. These are decorative compositions, not official flag specifications.

Each composition pins both input file checksums, upstream source URLs/checksums, the flag crop, fitting mode and any field/frame adjustment. Flag IDs and internal references are namespaced. The importer rejects altered inputs and mismatches between a composition and the staged flag/silhouette. After reviewing input or placement changes, run `python3 scripts/state_flag_artwork.py`, `npm run logos:flags`, then `npm run teams:flags`; this reproduces the new compositions offline from the checked-in inputs. Existing flags, silhouettes and saved alternate IDs remain intact.

NHL identity and conference data were checked against the NHL standings feed on 2026-10-18. All 32 primary logos come from the NHL's `assets.nhle.com` SVG collection, with source checksums and source palettes recorded in `nhl-manifest.json`. The Utah entry is the Mammoth. Six primary marks that spell their identity are flagged `showsName`, keeping them out of random logo pools while available for manual selection. Team marks remain their owners' trademarks.

A few detailed state seals exceed the former 400 KB limit; the flag importer allows up to 750 KB per vector, preserving the original artwork. The limit for other imported collections remains 400 KB. North Dakota's source contains an empty text node, removed explicitly; no live fonts or embedded rasters are shipped.

# WNBA artwork

WNBA identities, conferences and team codes were checked against wnba.com's team data and ESPN's 2026 standings on 2026-10-22: 15 active teams, seven in the East and eight in the West, including the 2026 expansion Portland Fire and Toronto Tempo. Cleveland, which the WNBA lists as inactive until 2028, is left out. All 15 primary logos come from the WNBA's `cdn.wnba.com` SVG collection, imported without edits, with source checksums and source palettes recorded in `wnba-manifest.json`. Team colors come from the same team data; where a listed color never appears in the logo (the Aces' red, the Fire's black, the Valkyries' and Mystics' header shades), the team's button color, or for the Aces the logo's own black and silver, is used instead. The Mystics and Fever primaries spell their names and are flagged `showsName`. Team marks remain their owners' trademarks.
